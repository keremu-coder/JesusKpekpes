Stick centre — check this on a real DualSense Edge

Do this on the PC you play on, with the Edge on a data USB cable and DS4Windows open.
Hands off both sticks for every read. Bluetooth is supposed to be refused. Nothing in this list saves to the controller's flash.

1. Plug the Edge in over USB. Open Jesus Kpekpes, Stick centre. If the text says Bluetooth, the cable is charge-only or the pad is paired over radio. Fix that before anything else.
2. If the text names HidHide, add JesusKpekpes.exe in HidHide's Applications list. Do not remove the Edge from HidHide's devices. This program does not change HidHide.
3. Press Read resting sticks (5 s). Leave the sticks alone. Copy the whole text.
   The left and right lines should say either a wrong centre, a worn stick, both, or already at the centre.
   Note the report rate and the worst gap.
4. Press Apply centre in memory. Copy the whole text, including the before and after rate.
   If the rate dropped or the worst gap grew by more than 10%, the text must say the backup was written back. Play a minute and confirm the camera is as it was.
   If it kept the write, confirm the steady spin is gone or smaller, then close the window and play. The program must not still have the pad open.
5. Unplug the Edge and plug it back into the same USB port. Open Stick centre again. The stored centre should be written once, then the handle closes. Copy that text.
6. Press Restore backup. The rest point should go back to the pre-write values. Copy the text. The controller should not ask you to re-pair, and DS4Windows should still see the pad.
7. Optional: connect the Edge over Bluetooth on purpose. The window must refuse it and must not write.

What this PC could not check: a real Edge, DS4Windows, and HidHide were not attached here. The checks that ran are the ones in tests/StickChecks.cs. Steps 1 to 7 are the hardware pass.
