The window has two separate parts.

Audio tune. Drop and Well are the two footstep mixes. Stereo is the default. Turn it off and the rest of your audio, including Rara, is left in place. Keep my other audio setup is on by default.

PC tune. What will change lists the PC and controller settings before anything is written. Optimize does not touch audio. Undo puts those PC settings back and leaves audio alone.
